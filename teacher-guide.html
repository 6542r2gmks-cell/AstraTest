The Great Refinery Run · V2 partner-review release

Learning goal

Students explain how a sequence of processing, testing, and delivery steps turns crude oil into a useful product. They connect one game decision to a real engineering purpose.

A suggested lesson

Use this as a planning template, not a measured game-completion time. Adjust for reading level, device familiarity, and classroom support. Completing all 16 units is a multi-session option.

  1. Before play: ask and predict.Where does jet fuel come from? Why might a refinery need more than one kind of equipment?
  2. During play: follow one product.Start with extraction. At the distillation tower, choose a product and follow its route. Pause for unfamiliar terms.
  3. After play: explain one decision.Ask learners to name an action, its purpose, and what happened when it was done incorrectly.
  4. Extend: choose another pathway.After delivery the control map opens. Compare a different fuel or explore sulfur recovery.

Pair-play roles

Operator: controls the game and describes each action. Investigator: reads targets, watches results, and asks why. Swap roles at the next unit. Both learners should explain one decision in their own words.

Quick check for understanding

Ask each learner to complete: “We used _____ to _____. If we skipped that step, _____.” A game completion badge records the activity; use the explanation to assess understanding.

Controls and access

Tap/click to act, hold additive controls for continuous dosing, and drag objects to their destination. Keyboard controls are available for buttons and interactive targets. In alkylation and FCC, the alternative-control panel also supports selecting an object and then its destination. The coker offers an alternate nozzle control.

Use Pause & help to discuss an activity. Page text can be enlarged. Motion reduction removes decorative effects while keeping motion needed to play. Test your actual devices and any assistive technology before class.

Shared devices and progress

Progress belongs to a browser, not a named student. Decide whether a pair or class is working toward one shared passport. To begin a separate learner’s record, clear v2 progress. Export a progress copy first if you want to keep the earlier work. A new delivery run preserves completed units.

Scientific scope

This is a simplified educational model. Game component properties, operating ranges, costs, and pass/fail limits illustrate relationships; they are not plant-design data, universal fuel requirements, or operating procedures. Use the game’s facts as a discussion starting point and review advanced claims for your curriculum.

Partner review before classroom adoption

Confirm the v2 build and unit requirements against the version referenced by your curriculum. Run a complete chosen route, verify touch input on your devices, test a return after closing the browser, and check printing. See release-notes.html and the included partner checklist.
